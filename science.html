Biology

Cells

Notes:

Cells are the basic units of life. Plant and animal cells have different structures.

Example:

Plant cells have chloroplasts for photosynthesis; animal cells do not.

Practice:
  1. Name one structure found only in plant cells.
  2. What is the function of mitochondria?
  3. Which cell part controls the cell?
Show answers
  1. Chloroplasts / cell wall.
  2. Respiration / energy release.
  3. Nucleus.
