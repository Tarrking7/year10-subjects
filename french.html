Vocabulary

School

Notes:

Key words: le collège (school), le professeur (teacher), les devoirs (homework).

Example:

“J’aime le français parce que c’est intéressant.”

Practice:
  1. Translate: “I hate homework.”
  2. Translate: “The teacher is kind.”
  3. Write one sentence about school.
