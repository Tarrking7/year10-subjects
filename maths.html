Fractions & Percentages

Notes:

Convert between fractions, decimals and percentages using division and ×100.

Example:

Convert 7/8 to a percentage: 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875 → 87.5%

Practice:
  1. Write 0.36 as a fraction.
  2. Increase 120 by 12%.
  3. Convert 3/20 to a decimal.
Show answers
  1. 36/100 = 9/25
  2. 120 × 1.12 = 134.4
  3. 0.15
