Language Techniques

Persuasive Devices

Notes:

Persuasive writing uses emotive language, rhetorical questions, repetition and statistics.

Example:

“Over 70% of students feel stressed — we must act now.”

Practice:
  1. Write one sentence using emotive language.
  2. Write a rhetorical question about school rules.
  3. Identify the persuasive device: “Everyone knows homework is essential.”
Show answers
  1. Any emotional sentence.
  2. Any rhetorical question.
  3. Appeal to popularity.
